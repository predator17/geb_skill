# Repo Audit for Full Book Coverage

## Current State
- High-resolution annotations: MIU, pq-system, Contracrostipunctus.
- Scripts with tests: miu.py (tested), pq.py (now tested).
- Unit map and SKILL.md support the entire book structure.
- Full page-marked text store: not fully committed (size constraints on interactive push).

## Scale Assessment
Complete high-resolution annotations (word/sentence/concept/author-intention level with empirical verification) for all 20+ chapters and 800+ pages exceeds practical limits of a single interactive session due to token volume and verification requirements.

## Recommended Split
Continue in sequential batches of 3-5 units at a time, pushing after each batch. The framework is ready for incremental expansion.
