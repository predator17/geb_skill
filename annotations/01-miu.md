# Unit: Chapter I — The MU-puzzle (MIU-system)
**PDF pages**: 77–85  
**Printed folios**: ~33–41

## Key terms
- **formal system** (PDF p. 77): Symbols + rules producing theorems from axioms.
- **MIU-system**: Letters M, I, U only.
- **axiom**: MI.
- **theorem**: String produced by rules.
- **derivation**: Explicit production sequence.

## Concept unpacking
- Inside vs Outside: Applying rules vs noticing invariants (all theorems start with M; I-count mod 3 ≠ 0).
- Decision procedure: Finite terminating test for theoremhood.
- MU is impossible by the mod-3 invariant.

## Author-stated intentions
Hofstadter wants the reader to experience working inside a formal system and then jumping outside to gain insight.
