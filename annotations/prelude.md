## Key terms
- **prelude**: The opening section of a musical work, here used as the title and structural model.
- **Fermat’s Last Theorem**: The famous unsolved (at the time) claim that no positive integers a, b, c satisfy aⁿ + bⁿ = cⁿ for n > 2.
- **attacca**: A musical direction meaning to proceed immediately to the next section.

## Concept unpacking
- Achilles and the Tortoise visit the Crab and introduce the Anteater; they present a record whose existence is linked to a mathematical result.
- The dialogue sets up the themes of the following Ant Fugue and discusses the relationship between preludes and fugues.
- It is based on preludes and fugues from Bach’s Well-Tempered Clavier.

## Author-stated intentions
- This Dialogue attaches to the next one. They are based on preludes and fugues from Bach’s Well-Tempered Clavier.
- Themes from the Prelude return, transformed, at the end of the Ant Fugue.

## Connections to thesis
- Introduces the interplay of music, mathematics, and levels of description that continues in the Ant Fugue.
- Prepares the discussion of holism and reductionism.
