**PDF pages**: 171+

## Key terms
- **recursion**: Nesting and variations on nesting; defining something in terms of simpler versions of itself.
- **push / pop / stack**: Mechanisms for suspending a task, taking up a simpler one, and later resuming exactly where one left off.
- **Recursive Transition Network (RTN)**: A diagram showing paths that can be followed, including recursive calls, used to describe linguistic and other structures.

## Concept unpacking
- Recursion appears in music, language, stories, and formal systems.
- A properly formulated recursive definition never leads to infinite regress because it always bottoms out in simpler cases.
- Stacks keep track of return addresses and variable bindings so that each level can be resumed correctly.

## Author-stated intentions
- To present the idea of recursion in many different contexts: musical patterns, linguistic patterns, and formal systems.
- To formalize the intuition introduced in the Little Harmonic Labyrinth Dialogue.

## Connections to thesis
- Recursion is a fundamental generative mechanism underlying hierarchical complexity and strange loops.
- Links music, language, formal systems, and computation.
