# Unit: Central Dogma of Typogenetics (and mapping to TNT / real genetics)
**PDF pages**: 556+
**Printed folio**: ~513

## Key terms
- **Central Dogma of Typogenetics**: Strands define enzymes (via the Typogenetic Code); enzymes act back on the strands, creating new information.
- **tangled hierarchy**: Neither strands nor enzymes are strictly higher-level; information flows in both directions.
- **Central Dogmap**: The analogy between the Typogenetics / molecular biology diagram and the relationship between TNT and its metalanguage.

## Concept unpacking
- In Typogenetics the two-way street between strands and enzymes shows a mixture of levels (data and program roles).
- In ordinary formal systems (MIU, TNT) the rules of inference sit strictly above the strings.
- The diagram of TNT and its metalanguage resembles the Central Dogma of Molecular Biology, revealing a profound kinship.

## Author-stated intentions
- To show how Typogenetics illustrates a tangled hierarchy and prepares the comparison with real genetics and with formal systems that can talk about themselves.
- To use the conceptual skeleton of Typogenetics as a guide to the labyrinth of true genetics.

## Connections to thesis
- Strange loops appear in both molecular biology and in formal systems capable of self-reference.
- Links the book’s formal, biological, and cognitive themes.
