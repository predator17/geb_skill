**PDF pages**: 724+

## Key terms
- **canon**: A musical form in which one voice imitates another, often with transformations.
- **augmentation**: A transformation in which notes are lengthened (here, verbally realized).
- **inversion**: A transformation in which the melody is turned upside-down (here, verbally and on the piano).
- **Sloth**: The character who hangs upside-down, embodying the inversion theme.

## Concept unpacking
- Achilles and the Tortoise visit the Sloth and discuss races (Zeno and Carroll).
- The dialogue features an inverted piano and verbal versions of musical transformations.
- It is based on a Bach canon from the Musical Offering.

## Author-stated intentions
- A canon which imitates a Bach canon in which one voice plays the same melody as another, only upside down and twice as slowly.
- The dialogue employs verbal versions of both inversion and augmentation.

## Connections to thesis
- Illustrates isomorphism between musical canons and linguistic structures.
- Continues the exploration of transformations, levels, and self-reference.
