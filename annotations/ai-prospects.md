# Unit: Chapter XIX — Artificial Intelligence: Prospects
**PDF pages**: 684+
**Printed folio**: ~641

## Key terms
- **subjunctive instant replay / Contrafactus**: Exploring “almost” situations and counterfactual worlds.
- **layered knowledge representation**: How knowledge is represented in layers of context.

## Concept unpacking
- The chapter is triggered by the Contrafactus dialogue, which explores how changes in context or rules produce radically different outcomes.
- AI must handle not only actual situations but also the space of nearby possible worlds and the knowledge required to reason about them.
- Subjunctives and counterfactuals reveal the complexity of representing flexible, context-sensitive knowledge.

## Author-stated intentions
- To discuss how knowledge is represented in layers of context and what this implies for artificial intelligence.
- To examine the prospects for AI in light of the difficulties of handling flexible, almost-situations.

## Connections to thesis
- Links levels of description, active symbols, and the difficulty of capturing human-like flexibility in formal systems.
- Continues the book’s exploration of mind, computation, and the limits of mechanical intelligence.
