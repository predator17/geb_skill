**PDF pages**: 676+

## Key terms
- **counterfactual**: A hypothetical variant on a real situation; the central theme of the dialogue.
- **subjunctive**: The grammatical mood used for hypotheticals; here applied to mental organization.
- **Bicyclops**: A playful invented concept illustrating the dialogue’s theme of near-misses and variants.

## Concept unpacking
- The Crab hosts friends to watch a football game; the conversation is filled with invented near-variants of ordinary objects and situations.
- The dialogue explores how we unconsciously organize thoughts to imagine hypothetical alternatives.
- It is connected to the later discussion of “almost” situations in the Artificial Intelligence chapter.

## Author-stated intentions
- About how we unconsciously organize our thoughts so that we can imagine hypothetical variants on real situations.
- The dialogue illustrates the mental manufacture of variants on ordinary concepts.

## Connections to thesis
- Explores the flexibility and structure of mental representations.
- Links everyday thought, formal systems, and the nature of intelligence.
