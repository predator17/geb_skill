# Unit: Dialogue — Edifying Thoughts of a Tobacco Smoker
**PDF pages**: 523+
**Printed folio**: ~480

## Key terms
- **self-replication**: The process by which a system produces a copy of itself.
- **distributed information**: Information for self-assembly spread across multiple parts rather than localized in one place.
- **Magritte**: The Belgian surrealist painter whose works appear in the dialogue.

## Concept unpacking
- Achilles visits the Crab and they discuss Magritte paintings that explore paradox and illusion.
- The dialogue treats problems connected with self-replication and the distribution of information.
- It is based on a Bach poem of the same title.

## Author-stated intentions
- A Dialogue treating of many topics, with the thrust being problems connected with self-replication.
- The concept of distributed information for self-assembly is illustrated by the Crab’s methods.

## Connections to thesis
- Explores self-reference and self-replication in artistic and biological terms.
- Links visual art, music, and formal systems.
