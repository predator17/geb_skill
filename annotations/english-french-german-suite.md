## Key terms
- **Jabberwocky**: Lewis Carroll’s nonsense poem used as the basis for the interlude.
- **translation**: The rendering of the poem into French and German, illustrating isomorphism across languages.
- **suite**: A musical form consisting of a sequence of movements; here applied to the parallel texts.

## Concept unpacking
- The interlude presents Lewis Carroll’s “Jabberwocky” alongside French and German translations.
- It demonstrates how meaning and structure can be preserved (or playfully distorted) across languages.
- The form is a light interlude between heavier chapters.

## Author-stated intentions
- An interlude consisting of Lewis Carroll’s nonsense poem “Jabberwocky” together with two translations.
- The relation between concepts and neural activity is discussed in the surrounding material.

## Connections to thesis
- Illustrates isomorphism and the preservation of structure across different systems (languages).
- Continues the book’s exploration of meaning, form, and translation.
