# Unit: Chapter XI — Brains and Thoughts
**PDF pages**: 380+
**Printed folio**: ~337

## Key terms
- **intensionality vs extensionality**: Descriptions can float without being anchored to specific objects; thought is intensional.
- **active symbols**: In the brain, symbols are active elements that store, transmit, and receive information (unlike passive typographical symbols).
- **calculus of descriptions**: The ability to invent, merge, split, and manipulate mental descriptions.

## Concept unpacking
- Computers and formal systems provide a new perspective on thought by allowing experimentation with alien forms of thinking.
- Meaning arises from isomorphisms, but in the brain the isomorphism is flexible and multi-descriptonal rather than rigid one-to-one.
- The stage was set by the Prelude and Ant Fugue; this chapter develops the ideas more deeply by uniting AI insights with neuroscience and cognitive psychology.

## Author-stated intentions
- To explore how formal systems can serve as models for brain processes of idea manipulation.
- To distinguish the flexibility of intensional thought from rigid formal mappings.

## Connections to thesis
- Links levels of description, symbols, and the hardware of mind.
- Prepares the ground for later discussions of consciousness and strange loops.
