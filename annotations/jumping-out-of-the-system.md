# Unit: Chapter XV — Jumping out of the System
**PDF pages**: 508+
**Printed folio**: ~465

## Key terms
- **jumping out of the system**: The act of stepping outside a formal system or set of rules to reason about it or extend it.
- **TNT+G**: The system obtained by adding the Gödel sentence G as an additional axiom.
- **multifurcation**: The infinite branching that occurs when each extension of TNT receives its own new Gödel sentence.
- **repeatability of Gödel’s argument**: The fact that the incompleteness construction can be reapplied to any sufficiently powerful extension.

## Concept unpacking
- Adding G as an axiom plugs one hole but immediately creates a new undecidable sentence G′ about the enlarged system.
- The process can be repeated indefinitely, producing an infinite hierarchy of extensions, none of which is complete.
- The same phenomenon appears in discussions of intelligence, creativity, and the limits of mechanical procedures.

## Author-stated intentions
- To show the repeatability of Gödel’s argument and its implication that TNT is not only incomplete but essentially incomplete.
- To explore what it means for intelligence to jump out of the system in which it is operating.

## Connections to thesis
- Demonstrates that incompleteness cannot be escaped by simply adding axioms.
- Links formal incompleteness to questions about creativity, free will, and the nature of intelligence.
