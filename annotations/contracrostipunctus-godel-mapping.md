## Key terms
- **isomorphism**: Structural correspondence between the dialogue and Gödel's theorem.
- **boomeranging**: The self-destructive effect of the record / self-referential string.
- **high-fidelity vs low-fidelity**: Strong vs weak formal systems.

## Concept unpacking
- Each half of the dialogue is an isomorphic copy of Gödel's incompleteness result.
- The table maps phonograph → axiomatic system, record → string, unplayable record → true but unprovable statement, etc.
- The dialogue itself exhibits the self-reference it describes.

## Author-stated intentions
- The deepest reason for writing Contracrostipunctus was to illustrate Gödel's Theorem via two levels of meaning.
- Readers who notice the mapping have already tasted the flavor of the theorem.
