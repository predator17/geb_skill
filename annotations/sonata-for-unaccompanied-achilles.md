# Unit: Dialogue — Sonata for Unaccompanied Achilles
**PDF pages**: 105+
**Printed folio**: ~61

## Key terms
- **figure and ground**: Achilles’ spoken lines form the figure; the Tortoise’s implied replies form the ground.
- **unaccompanied**: Only one speaker is transcribed.
- **sonata**: Form imitated from Bach’s unaccompanied violin sonatas.

## Concept unpacking
- Achilles speaks on the telephone; only his side is recorded.
- The conversation concerns Escher’s figure-ground drawings and related ideas.
- The dialogue itself exemplifies the figure/ground distinction.

## Author-stated intentions
- Imitates the Bach Sonatas for unaccompanied violin.
- Achilles is the only speaker because it is a transcript of one end of a telephone call.
- The Dialogue forms an example of figure versus ground.

## Connections to thesis
- Prepares Chapter III on Figure and Ground.
- Shows that information can reside in what is absent as well as what is present.
