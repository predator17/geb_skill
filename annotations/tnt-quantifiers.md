# Unit: TNT — Quantifiers and Closed Formulas
**PDF pages**: 252+
**Printed folio**: ~208

## Key terms
- **quantifier**: ∃ (there exists) and ∀ (for all).
- **closed formula / sentence**: No free variables; has a truth value.
- **open formula**: Contains free variables; expresses a property (predicate).
- **quantified variable**: Bound by a quantifier.

## Concept unpacking
- Prefixing a quantifier turns an open formula into a sentence.
- Example: ∃b:(b+S0)=SS0 is true; ∀b:(b+S0)=SS0 is false.
- Completes the vocabulary for expressing number-theoretic statements in TNT.

## Author-stated intentions
- To give TNT the power to express universal and existential claims of number theory.
- Foundation for later Gödel numbering and self-reference.
