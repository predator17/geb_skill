# Unit: Dialogue — Chromatic Fantasy, And Feud
**PDF pages**: 221+
**Printed folio**: ~177

## Key terms
- **use-mention distinction**: The difference between using a word and mentioning the word itself.
- **chromatic**: Referring to chromatic scales in music; here a pun on color and musical form.
- **feud**: A quarrel or dispute; here between Achilles and the Tortoise over the color of the shell.

## Concept unpacking
- The dialogue is a short, playful exchange that appears to have little connection to Bach’s Chromatic Fantasy beyond the title.
- It revolves around a dispute about whether the Tortoise’s shell is green, illustrating subtleties of language and agreement.
- The form is light and conversational.

## Author-stated intentions
- A short Dialogue bearing hardly any resemblance, except in title, to Bach’s Chromatic Fantasy and Fugue.
- The relationship of intelligence to “absolute” meaning is postulated in the surrounding discussion.

## Connections to thesis
- Explores the limits of language and meaning in a light form.
- Prepares themes of interpretation and levels of description.
