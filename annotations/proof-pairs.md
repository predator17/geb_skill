# Unit: Proof-Pairs (Chapter XIV — On Formally Undecidable Propositions)
**PDF pages**: 481+
**Printed folio**: ~438

## Key terms
- **proof-pair**: A pair of natural numbers (m, n) where m is the Gödel number of a derivation whose bottom line has Gödel number n.
- **TNT-proof-pair / MIU-proof-pair**: The notion applied to each formal system.
- **two key ideas of the oyster**: (1) TNT can talk about its own strings via Gödel-numbering (introspection); (2) this self-scrutiny can be concentrated into a single string (focusing trick / Cantor diagonal).

## Concept unpacking
- Being a proof-pair is a primitive recursive number-theoretic property and can therefore be expressed inside TNT.
- This allows statements like “this string is a theorem” to be translated into pure number theory.
- The chapter presents the intuitive core of Gödel’s 1931 proof.

## Author-stated intentions
- To stress the two key ideas at the heart of the proof rather than the fully technical machinery.
- To show how the Gödel isomorphism turns typographical rules into arithmetical rules.

## Connections to thesis
- Foundation for constructing the self-referential sentence G that asserts its own unprovability.
