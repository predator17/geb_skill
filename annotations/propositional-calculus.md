# Unit: The Propositional Calculus
**PDF pages**: 225+
**Printed folio**: ~181

## Key terms
- **Propositional Calculus**: Formal system for propositional reasoning (and, or, not, if-then).
- **atoms**: P, Q, R and primed variants.
- **well-formed strings**: Defined recursively by formation rules.
- **Rule of Joining**: If x and y are theorems, then <x ∧ y> is a theorem.

## Concept unpacking
- Symbols do what ordinary words ("and", "or", "not", "if...then") are supposed to do, purely typographically.
- Semi-interpretation yields universally true sentences.
- Contrasts with systems that have both lengthening and shortening rules.

## Author-stated intentions
- To make symbols behave as Achilles wished the Tortoise's words would behave.
- Foundation for later extension into TNT.
