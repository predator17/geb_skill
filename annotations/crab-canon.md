# Unit: Dialogue — Crab Canon
**PDF pages**: 243+
**Printed folio**: ~199

## Key terms
- **crab canon**: A musical form in which one voice plays the theme forwards while the other plays it backwards; named for the sideways motion of crabs.
- **palindrome**: A sequence that reads the same forwards and backwards.
- **indirect self-reference**: Self-reference achieved through structure or form rather than direct statement.

## Concept unpacking
- The dialogue is constructed so that the second half is a near-exact reverse of the first half, mirroring the structure of a musical crab canon.
- It interweaves Gödel, Escher, and Bach through formal trickery, visual art, music, and wordplay.
- The form itself is an example of the content it discusses.

## Author-stated intentions
- To create a dialogue based on the crab canon from Bach’s Musical Offering.
- To provide a dense interweaving of the book’s three central figures through structural self-reference.

## Connections to thesis
- Exemplifies strange loops and self-reference at the level of form.
- Connects musical form, visual art (Escher), and formal systems.
