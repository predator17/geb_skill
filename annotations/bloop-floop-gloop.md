# Unit: Chapter XIII — BlooP and FlooP and GlooP
**PDF pages**: 449–455
**Printed folios**: ~406–412

## Key terms
- **BlooP**: A language whose only control structure is the bounded loop; it can express only predictably terminating calculations (primitive recursive functions).
- **primitive recursive truths**: The core truths of number theory that involve only predictably terminating calculations; their representability is the criterion for a system being “sufficiently powerful.”
- **Gantö’s Ax**: The observation that a system is incomplete either because it is powerful enough for Gödel’s method or because it is not powerful enough to capture all primitive recursive truths.

## Concept unpacking
BlooP is introduced to make precise the notion of a predictably long search. Its hallmark is the bounded loop whose upper bound is fixed before the loop begins. Any function definable in BlooP is primitive recursive. TNT is shown to be sufficiently powerful because it can represent all primitive recursive truths as theorems; therefore Gödel’s method applies and TNT is incomplete. The same idea appears metaphorically in the high-fidelity requirement of the Contracrostipunctus.

## Author-stated intentions
To clarify the notions of primitive and general recursivity, to link self-reference in TNT to questions of order versus chaos among the natural numbers, and to prepare the formal machinery used in the proof of incompleteness.
