# Unit: Dialogue — Air on G’s String
**PDF pages**: 474+
**Printed folio**: ~431

## Key terms
- **quining**: The operation of preceding a phrase by its own quotation, used to achieve self-reference.
- **yields falsehood when preceded by its quotation**: The classic Quine sentence that asserts its own falsehood.
- **self-reference via quotation**: A linguistic method for constructing self-referential statements.

## Concept unpacking
- The dialogue mirrors Gödel’s self-referential construction using ordinary language and the technique of quining.
- It shows how a sentence can talk about itself by referring to its own quotation.
- The form is light and conversational while introducing a key technical idea.

## Author-stated intentions
- To present a dialogue in which Gödel’s self-referential construction is mirrored in words.
- The idea is attributed to Quine.

## Connections to thesis
- Provides an accessible linguistic analogue of Gödel’s arithmetical self-reference.
- Bridges formal systems and ordinary language.
